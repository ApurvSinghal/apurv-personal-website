## My positions

1. Most enterprise AI initiatives fail in production not because of the LLM, but because nobody built the telemetry, compliance boundaries, or CI/CD pipelines to support them.
2. If your deployment pipeline requires manual checklists or takes 45 minutes, you don't have a platform; you have friction.
3. Passing raw PII into LLMs or decision systems is an architectural bug. Enforce zero-PII boundaries at the ingestion perimeter before data is persisted.
4. From December 2026, Australian privacy law requires automated decisions to be transparent. If you don't have cryptographic, tamper-evident logs of algorithmic decisions, you won't survive an audit.
5. Real engineering is about making complex distributed systems boringly reliable, observable, and compliant.
